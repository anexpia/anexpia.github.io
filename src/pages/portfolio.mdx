## About me
Hello! I'm a proficient scripter with 5+ years of experience.
I first began with building late 2019 and later learned scripting - around April 2020 - as it was a necessity for me to achieve what I wished to create with my builds at the time. Later on, I found my passion in scripting.

I excel at writing maintainable and efficient code, I'm quick at adapting to unfamiliar code, reading and organizing messy codebases, and learning new concepts whenever needed.\
My main focus is designing and making backend systems as well as plugins, though I can do most of everything else.

### Public / Open Source
- [BufferEncoder](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/bufferencoder-very-efficient-table-to-buffer-serializer-that-doesn’t-use-schemas/3584699?silent=true) (Module)\
This is a module that serializes any form of table you provide to it into a buffer as efficiently as possible without relying on schemas unlike the majority of the other publicly available serializers. 
It supports all datatypes you'd realistically have in a table and can be easily modified for any of your needs.
--- 

- [DevRoam](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/devroam-move-around-with-your-character-as-if-playtesting/3753226?silent=true) (Plugin)\
This is a free plugin that lets you move around in studio with your own character, It attempts to mimic regular movement and camera behavior in edit mode as much as possible while providing extra handy features.

- [Squish](https://github.com/anexpia/Squish) (Module)\
This is a heavily modified fork of [Squash](https://github.com/Data-Oriented-House/Squash).\
It comes with more types of SerDes (eg. instances, deduplication), and is more optimized.

- [MoreEditors](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/8730655360/MoreEditors) (Plugin)\
A plugin that adds draggers for beams, lights, stellar bodies (sun & moon), and moving objects along 2 axes.

- [TextWrapper](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/textwrapper-a-module-to-wrap-text-into-lines-similar-to-textlabels/3637151?silent=true) (Module)\
This is a module that splits provided text into multiple lines efficiently.
It supports rich text and can also provide offsets of characters to find within the text

- [Instance Saver](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/12680363527/PSCP-RoomSaver-5) (Plugin) \
<small>This one is mainly intended for loading custom builds within the game I work for. It cannot be used for anything else unless modified.</small> <br />
It's an instance serializer that can perfectly serialize and deserialize instances while saving as much bandwidth as possible with compression and encryption of the data.

- [Unicode Text Filter](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/filter-out-unicode-characters-used-to-bypass-chat-filter/3659694) (Module)\
Replaces almost all instances of unicode text used for bypassing in chat with the regular characters in text.\
It relies on Unicode.org's [ConfusablesSummary](https://www.unicode.org/Public/security/latest/confusablesSummary.txt) and is made to be future-compatible with any new updates to it, so long they don't change its format.
